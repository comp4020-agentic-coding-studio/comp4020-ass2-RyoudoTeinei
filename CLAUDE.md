# How to Leave: working agreement

## The course we are making

Teach ordinary subscribers to find the biller, read the commitment, follow the
appropriate cancellation route, and verify the result. No programming experience
is required. Do not drift into a course about designing cancellation interfaces.
The user's revision from UX engineering to consumer practice is the curriculum's
central decision. Apple, Google Play, Adobe and Netflix are teaching cases;
STAY+ is a clearly fictional practice service, not the subject of every week.

## Course continuity

- Keep the allocated code suffix 954. Course dates and identity live in
  `src/course-config.ts`; schedule and assessment displays read the collections.
- Twelve dated lectures and twelve linked workshops must form a progression:
  inventory and terms, platform cases, confirmation and problem-solving.
- Every week needs a measurable learning outcome, an activity with supplied
  materials, and a usable output. Explain a skill before assessing it.
- Each platform practice lesson includes a concise, scoped action sequence,
  not only a discussion prompt. Label pre-request outcomes as proposed; only
  verified account evidence may be presented as the completed result.
- Assessment is inventory 25%, a sourced field guide 30%, and an unfamiliar-case
  practical 45%. All deadlines use the named Canberra teaching calendar.
- Offer simulated data for every task; a student need not buy, cancel, or expose
  a real subscription. The site never asks for account credentials or payment data.

## Factual discipline

- Platform instructions cite first-party sources. Keep region, plan, purchase
  channel and verification date beside the guidance. An old screenshot is not
  evidence of a current interface.
- Keep regulator allegations, a proposed settlement, a company's response, and
  current help instructions distinguishable. Do not turn the Adobe case into a
  blanket claim that every cancellation fee is unlawful or refundable.
- Paying monthly is not necessarily a monthly commitment. Cancelling, pausing,
  deleting an app, requesting a refund and stopping renewal are different acts.
- Do not promise fee waivers or teach unverified plan-switching loopholes.
- Paraphrase briefly, link to original sources, and write our own exercises.
  Do not invent customer testimonials, completed studies or student results.

## Design and delivery

- Preserve the starter's SlopU branding/palette, four collection keys, schemas,
  generated API and Astro integration pipeline described in README.md.
- Use the theme's base-aware URLs. Extend the course pages and components within
  that platform; do not replace it with a separate stack or hosting service.
- English learner-facing copy. Large, readable typography; clear editorial
  hierarchy; restrained Slop gold, bronze, warm neutrals and black/white.
- The practice lab is optional, keyboard-operable and resettable. It must give
  explanatory feedback and distinguish a pending request from a completed one.
- Keep human judgement explicit: tests can check weights, dates, outcomes,
  sources and state transitions, not whether a lesson is persuasive or fair.
- Make meaningful commits as the work develops. Run appropriate checks at each
  stage, and inspect the actual site at 1920x1080 and 390x844 before handoff.
- PROCESS.md records only decisions and verification that actually happened,
  with real commit links. It is a reviewable draft for the student's own account.
