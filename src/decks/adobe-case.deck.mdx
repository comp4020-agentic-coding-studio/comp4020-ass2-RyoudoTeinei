How to Leave · Week 6 · Adobe case

## Two clocks

**Payment frequency** tells you when money moves.

**Commitment length** tells you the period you agreed to.

Record both before choosing an action.

---

## Read the practice card

Mosaic Studio · fictional teaching plan

20 per month · 12-month commitment

6 payments remain.

Practice early-exit rule: half the remaining balance.

---

## Show the calculation

6 × 20 × 0.5 = **60**

This is a calculation from supplied fictional terms.

It is not a quote for a real account.
