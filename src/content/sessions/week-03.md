## Before the workshop

Read this week's lecture and open the [practice pack and worksheets](/resources/). Bring a browser and a way to write notes. Use the fictional account aliases; no personal records are needed.

## Work the case

Compare the monthly STAY+ plan with Mosaic Studio's annual commitment paid monthly. Annotate payment frequency, commitment and the printed exit condition separately.

Draw one payment timeline for each plan, then answer the four questions in the lecture. Discuss the difference between stopping renewal and requesting money back.

## What you leave with

Two annotated plan cards and a short explanation of remaining access.
