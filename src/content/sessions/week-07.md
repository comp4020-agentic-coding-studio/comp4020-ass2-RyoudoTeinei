## Before the workshop

Read this week's lecture and open the [practice pack and worksheets](/resources/). Bring a browser and a way to write notes. Use the fictional account aliases; no personal records are needed.

## Work the case

Use dossier D01: the design plan is the intended cancellation; the stock plan is to remain active. The first simulated request times out and the second returns confirmation C-204.

Explain each state before moving on. Complete the evidence sheet with C-204, renewal off, and the supplied access-end date. Check that you have not silently cancelled the stock plan.

## What you leave with

A completion record for the design plan and a separate status for the stock plan.
