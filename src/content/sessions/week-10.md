## Before the workshop

Read this week's lecture and open the [practice pack and worksheets](/resources/). Bring a browser and a way to write notes. Use the fictional account aliases; no personal records are needed.

## Work the case

Dossier D01 has a failed attempt at 09:14, confirmation at 09:18 and paid access ending 8 October. A later document names a different plan.

Put all events in order. Write a completed-state claim supported by its document ID. Separate any new charge that belongs to the other plan into a different investigation.

## What you leave with

A dated evidence sheet with a clear resolved/unresolved distinction.

<details>
<summary>Check your reasoning after attempting the task</summary>

The first attempt cannot establish success, and a record for one plan cannot explain every later charge.

</details>
