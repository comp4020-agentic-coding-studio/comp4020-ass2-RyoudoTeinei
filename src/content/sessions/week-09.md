## Before the workshop

Read this week's lecture and open the [practice pack and worksheets](/resources/). Bring a browser and a way to write notes. Use the fictional account aliases; no personal records are needed.

## Work the case

Use the three fictional offers in the resource pack: one-month pause, discount with a new commitment, or cancellation. Choose for a customer who no longer uses the service.

Write the original goal first. Compare future dates, amounts and commitments. Give a different answer for a second customer who still needs the service but wants a lower cost.

## What you leave with

A comparison table and two short, evidence-based decisions.

<details>
<summary>Check your reasoning after attempting the task</summary>

Declining every offer is not the assessment criterion. Explaining whether the offer meets the stated goal is.
