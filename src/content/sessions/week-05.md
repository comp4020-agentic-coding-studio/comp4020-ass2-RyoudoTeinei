## Before the workshop

Read this week's lecture and open the [practice pack and worksheets](/resources/). Bring a browser and a way to write notes. Use the fictional account aliases; no personal records are needed.

## Work the case

Three fictional customers want different things: permanent departure, a one-month break, and continued use on a new phone. Use the Google Play reading to separate the actions.

For each goal, select an action and write its expected result. For the pause example, use the supplied practice condition that payments resume on 8 November.

## What you leave with

A goal/action/result table with a resumption date wherever needed.

Deleting the app does not answer the billing question. The selected action must match the customer's stated goal.
