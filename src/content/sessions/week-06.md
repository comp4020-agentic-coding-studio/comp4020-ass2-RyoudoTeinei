## Before the workshop

Read this week's lecture and open the [practice pack and worksheets](/resources/). Bring a browser and a way to write notes. Use the fictional account aliases; no personal records are needed.

## Work the case

Read the US terms, the DOJ announcement and Adobe's response. Use a different label for a plan condition, an allegation and a company statement.

Make a four-entry source timeline. Then calculate the fee on the fictional Mosaic card: six payments of 20 remain; its printed practice rule is half the remaining commitment. Finish by listing which facts a real account would still require.

## What you leave with

A scoped case note and a shown calculation, with uncertainty made explicit.

<details>
<summary>Check your reasoning after attempting the task</summary>

The practice calculation is 60. It is not a quotation for a real Adobe customer in another region or plan.

</details>
