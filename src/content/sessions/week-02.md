## Before the workshop

Read this week's lecture and open the [practice pack and worksheets](/resources/). Bring a browser and a way to write notes. Use the fictional account aliases; no personal records are needed.

## Work the case

Use R01 and R02 to build a routing diagram. The service name alone cannot choose the cancellation channel.

Work individually for 15 minutes, then exchange diagrams. Your partner must point to the receipt field supporting every arrow. Revise any unsupported branch.

## What you leave with

Two evidence-backed routes and one unresolved-information question.
