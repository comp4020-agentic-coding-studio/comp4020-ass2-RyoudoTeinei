## Before the workshop

Read this week's lecture and open the [practice pack and worksheets](/resources/). Bring a browser and a way to write notes. Use the fictional account aliases; no personal records are needed.

## Work the case

In dossier S01, there is a cancellation request but no completion record. The goal is to confirm whether renewal is off before the stated next billing date.

Draft a message of at most 180 words naming the plan, purchase channel, attempt time, evidence and requested resolution. Your partner lists missing information; revise once.

## What you leave with

A concise message, an evidence index and a follow-up date.

<details>
<summary>Check your reasoning after attempting the task</summary>

A useful request makes its desired outcome explicit and does not invent an approval or refund.

</details>
