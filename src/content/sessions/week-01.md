## Before the workshop

Read this week's lecture and open the [practice pack and worksheets](/resources/). Bring a browser and a way to write notes. Use the fictional account aliases; no personal records are needed.

## Work the case

Sort the four receipts in the practice pack. Separate recurring services from the one-off purchase before recording any next action.

Spend 10 minutes marking evidence, 25 minutes completing the inventory, 15 minutes comparing with a partner and 10 minutes correcting assumptions.

## What you leave with

A four-row inventory with receipt IDs, named billers, account aliases and explicit unknowns.
