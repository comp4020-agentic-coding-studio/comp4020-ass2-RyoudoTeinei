## Before the workshop

Read this week's lecture and open the [practice pack and worksheets](/resources/). Bring a browser and a way to write notes. Use the fictional account aliases; no personal records are needed.

## Work the case

Use the Weekend Reader final dossier and the exit-practical brief. Start from the receipt rather than a memorised platform interface.

Spend 15 minutes on identification, 20 on route and terms, 15 on the completion record, and 10 explaining the limits of your conclusion to a partner.

## What you leave with

Your final practical document, checklist and declared assistance.
