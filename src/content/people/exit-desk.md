The Exit Desk is the teaching-team role in this fictional SlopU course. Bring a question about a receipt, a source or a confusing result to the weekly workshop. We will ask what the evidence says before asking you to choose a button.

## Learning support

All activities can use the supplied practice records. If a visual exercise is inaccessible, use the text dossier or agree a spoken walkthrough with the tutor. See [policies and support](/policies/) for the course's submission and extension arrangements.

No real accounts are managed by this site. For an actual provider problem, follow the appropriate official support link in the [source register](/resources/#sources).
